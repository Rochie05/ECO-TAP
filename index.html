Three simple steps

A little effort can go a long way.

The ECO-TAP concept connects recycling with rewards and reuse, making each step easy to understand.

01

Bring it to a kiosk

Sort eligible recyclable bottles and place them in the appropriate kiosk section.

02

Collect points

In the concept, accepted deposits can earn points that residents may redeem for rewards.

03

Support reuse

Sorted materials can be tracked for collection and considered for recycling or upcycling.
